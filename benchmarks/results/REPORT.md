## Corpus and method

- Corpus version 1, seed `20261102`: 20 faults and 11 clean controls (11 held out).
- Provenance: Generated from parametrized templates (dataproof_benchmark.corpus) with a seed frozen before any evaluation, by the same author as the system under test: not independently authored.
- Tier: database (each case migrated into a fresh PostgreSQL database; no previews).
- `B0`: the migration ran. `B1`: reconciliation over the reviewed binding view (row counts, a key-set hash, refund and capture sums per order and currency). `B2`: DataProof's deterministic contracts C1 to C3.
- `B3` (B2 plus AI investigation on the same evidence) is **not run**: live model calls are a paid service that has not been approved (#7, #58).
