## Reading it

- **What the rules are:** patterns that often go wrong in data migrations, matched in the changed source. A match is a reason to look, not a fault: a rule cannot tell a correct ranged backfill from one that skips its boundary.
- **Optimistic recall:** the rules were written by the corpus's author with the corpus in view. They are generic, with no case-specific literal, but their recall here is an upper bound.
- **Coverage:** what neither arm sees is out of reach. The rules read Python and SQL in the changed migration and application files, never the bindings.
